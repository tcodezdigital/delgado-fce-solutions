What is an FCE

A structured assessment of physical ability

A Functional Capacity Evaluation, or FCE, is a structured assessment used to measure an individual's physical abilities and limitations. Depending on the referral question and the individual's condition, an evaluation may assess:

Strength and mobility

Lifting and carrying capacity

Pushing and pulling ability

Sitting, standing, and walking tolerance

Balance and positional tolerance

Endurance and activity tolerance

Work-related tasks

Daily activities

Consistency of observed performance

The goal is to clarify what an individual can do safely, where limitations exist, and how an injury affects overall function.

When to consider an FCE

An FCE may be appropriate when

The diagnosis is documented, but the functional impact remains unclear.

A client reports difficulty working or completing daily activities.

Objective measurements of physical ability are needed.

Questions remain about work capacity or physical restrictions.

The case involves ongoing functional limitations.

Additional clarity is needed regarding the effects of an injury.

When functional questions remain unanswered, objective assessment can provide valuable clarity.
