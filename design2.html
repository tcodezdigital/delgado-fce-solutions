Understanding the Assessment

What Is a Functional Capacity Evaluation?

A Functional Capacity Evaluation, or FCE, is a structured assessment used to measure an individual's physical abilities and limitations.

The goal is to clarify what an individual can do safely, where limitations exist, and how an injury affects overall function.

Is an FCE Appropriate?

When Should You Consider an FCE?

✓The diagnosis is documented, but the functional impact remains unclear
✓A client reports difficulty working or completing daily activities
✓Objective measurements of physical ability are needed
✓Questions remain about work capacity or physical restrictions
✓The case involves ongoing functional limitations
✓Additional clarity is needed regarding the effects of an injury

Common Questions

Functional Capacity Evaluation FAQs

What does a Functional Capacity Evaluation measure?

An FCE is a structured assessment used to measure physical abilities and limitations, including areas such as strength, mobility, lifting, carrying, positional tolerance, endurance, work-related tasks, and daily activities.

How can an FCE add context to a personal-injury case?

Functional testing can add objective information about what an individual is currently able to do, where limitations exist, and how an injury affects work capacity and daily functioning.

Are evaluations individualized?

Yes. Each evaluation is designed around the individual's medical history, reported limitations, physical demands, and functional needs.
